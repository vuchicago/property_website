Cook County Tax Comparison

How To Compare Cook County Property Taxes

Searchers often want a quick calculator, but a useful comparison starts with the property record, assessed value, property class, and nearby parcels that are genuinely similar.

1. Start With The Address Or PIN

Use the property address to find the Cook County PIN, then confirm the official property record. PIN-based matching is more reliable than address text alone because one address can have formatting differences across data sources.

2. Compare Similar Properties

Review nearby properties with similar class, building size, age, and location. A lower assessment on a truly comparable property can be a useful signal, while broad neighborhood averages are only a starting point.

3. Verify Before Filing

Appeal deadlines and filing rules change by township and agency. Always check official Cook County Assessor or Board of Review information before relying on any comparison for a filing decision.

Sources And Methodology

Cook County Tax Compare is designed around public Cook County property, assessment, and appeal information. The tool helps organize comparison signals, but final values, deadlines, exemptions, and filing requirements should be verified through official Cook County records.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about property taxes, appeals, and our tools.

The best way is to compare your property's assessed value per square foot to similar properties in your neighborhood using our Property Tax Tool. If your neighbors with similar homes are assessed significantly lower, you likely have grounds for an appeal.

Filing an appeal directly with the Cook County Assessor's Office is completely free. You do not need to hire a lawyer to file an appeal, especially for residential properties. Our tools can help you review public data, and our optional appeal help is a flat $99.

Appeal windows are open for specific townships at different times of the year, usually for a period of 30 days. Check our "Appeal Deadlines" section or the official Assessor's website to see if your township is currently open for appeals.

We use public Cook County data sources where available. Always verify final numbers, deadlines, and filing requirements with official county records before submitting an appeal.
