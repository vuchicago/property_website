Cook County Property Tax Appeal Help

Before filing, compare your assessment, check your township appeal window, and gather evidence that supports your position.

1. Compare Your Assessment

Start by reviewing your property against similar nearby properties. Look for differences in assessed value, building characteristics, location, and sale history.

Open Property Tax Tool

2. Check The Official Deadline

Cook County appeal periods vary by township. Always confirm your filing window with the official Cook County Assessor or Board of Review calendar.

View Official Calendar

3. Decide How To File

You can file directly with Cook County for free. If you want help organizing the appeal request, Invited testers can prepare and download an appeal packet for free. Optional DocuSign, printing and mailing fulfillment costs $49.

View Appeal Help

Important Limitation

No website or service can guarantee a lower assessment. The appeal outcome depends on the property, available evidence, county rules, and the reviewing agency.
