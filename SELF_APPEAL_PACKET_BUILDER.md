# Saved-property self-appeal workflow

Implemented 2026-11-03. Assessor only; individually owned single-family homes with one dwelling unit. No new database migration is required. State uses the existing versioned `appeal_packets` JSON and private `APPEAL_PACKETS` R2 binding (`cookcountystorage`).

## Owner flow

The free preparation workspace uses a popup card flow instead of a long intake form and checklist. It opens directly after selecting a saved property. Owners answer one question at a time; saved owner/property details and suggested comparisons are prefilled. When required, the front-photo-and-capture-date card appears immediately after occupancy, before contact details and appeal grounds. Uploads save even before complete intake, and a saved photo is not requested again. A skipped photo remains missing for filing, with a direct link from final review to add it. Continue, Back and Save and close persist the answers and current card in the existing packet state, with optimistic revision checks. Reloading resumes the saved card. Failed saves retain the visible answer for retry.

The workspace action card contains only its title and action: **Prepare my appeal** for a new draft, **Continue** for saved progress, then **Download Submission** and an adjacent **Edit** once the current packet PDF exists. Preparation copies are labeled separately and never presented as completed submissions. Edit uses the existing revision transition, preserves answers/evidence and opens the question cards; approval resets and the rebuilt packet gets a new version. Signing/mailing restrictions still apply. When available, Download Submission returns the current signed PDF. Previous files and activity stay in collapsed sections.

Evidence cards appear only for missing documents that apply to the answers. Uploads may be skipped and completed later. The final review card has one **Download PDF** action: it assembles the complete packet, including the configured official signature forms and supporting documents, when everything is ready. Otherwise it downloads the clearly labeled preparation copy listing what is still needed. Neither route requires checkout or starts DocuSign. Optional paid fulfillment follows the existing approval/payment flow.

Appeal comparison suggestions include only similar properties with a positive imported total assessed value strictly below the appealed property's imported total assessed value (`taxableValue`). Equal, higher and unknown valuations are excluded. Filtering happens before the 20-record snapshot limit and applies to the card list, default selections, API validation, evidence completion and generated PDF exhibit, including older drafts. Cards and reports display **implied market value = assessed value ÷ 0.10**, and selection/output is capped at five eligible comparisons in similarity order. Missing subject values produce no automatic suggestions; owners can upload their own evidence. This uses the imported assessment rather than the owner's requested market value and does not change the general property-analysis tool or historical PDFs. See [ASSESSOR_SUBMISSION_FORMAT.md](ASSESSOR_SUBMISSION_FORMAT.md) for the researched layout, source links, selection limitations and a fictional PDF preview.

1. Add/select a property in My account, then choose **Prepare my appeal**. A property link starts/resumes the current-year case; the workspace selector supports another assessment year. Repeated starts reuse the existing case. New IDs are deterministic per owner, saved property, and year. Every property shows Delete, including properties with packet records. Deleting the saved address retains its appeal records, packets and private documents. Addresses referenced by the older `self_appeal_cases` workflow remain protected by its existing foreign key; the API reports this instead of deleting that work.
2. Review the imported property snapshot (PINs, township, class, size, year built, prior appeal and available values). The imported assessment year is not certified; compare it with the current county notice. Choose the grounds, requested **fair market value** and explanation yourself. The preparation form does not ask for a verified deadline or a calendar check. The service manages the calendar for optional fulfillment.
3. Answer the occupancy/document questions. A rented single-family home remains eligible, but is not an owner-occupied residence. Up to five imported comparisons are suggested for owner review. A routine uniformity statement is drafted for review; owners supply their own correction facts and special circumstances. Assessment comparisons do not substitute for sale/appraisal evidence on a market-value claim.
4. The preparation desk imports and maps current official applications and attestations once per year. The service prefills the actual PDFs from the confirmed owner, contact and property information. Owners do not find blank forms or transfer details into them. Missing mailing details are requested only when a required field lacks them. See [OFFICIAL_FORMS_SETUP.md](OFFICIAL_FORMS_SETUP.md).
5. Owners upload only applicable personal evidence: required photos, a comparison/photo exhibit when needed, closing records, an existing appraisal, or correction/damage records. The property summary and selected comparison exhibit are generated automatically. An assessment notice upload is optional when the saved record is adequate. A photo without a visible date triggers preparation of the official photo-date attestation. Attestation facts, signatures and any required notarization still come from the owner. Ordinary DocuSign does not notarize.
6. Build a combined PDF: prefilled official forms first, owner statement, property record, selected comparisons, attachment index, then supporting uploads. The index records the template source and revision. Signatures remain blank until the owner reviews and signs. Free downloads do not require purchase. There is no pre-build checkbox asking the owner to research the applicable forms.
7. Optional **$49** fulfillment becomes available after approval. Existing verified Stripe payment is required **before any DocuSign envelope is created**. It includes signing, printing, assembly, postage and tracking. The desk checks placement, sends the invitation, syncs completed documents, obtains mailing authorization, and records dispatch/delivery. Postal delivery is distinct from county filing.

Signed and authorized packets wait for their township appeal window. The API resolves the current service calendar from the stored township and assessment year, independently of customer input and snapshot dates. It blocks dispatch before opening, after closing, or while dates are unpublished or cannot be matched confidently. It records the calendar window and source with the mailing proof. The fulfillment desk maintains the service calendar against the official Assessor schedule and performs physical printing and mailing; this does not add automatic postal dispatch or roll a packet into another assessment year.

## Limits and operational status

- **Live official PDF import is still required.** The Assessor's PDF host returned HTTP 403 for the current form downloads. The code contains no recreated government application. Until an administrator imports and reviews the actual templates, owners can save their information/evidence and download a free preparation copy; only the complete filing packet waits for the preparation desk. The copy lists missing items, is marked not ready to file, and does not change approval or payment status. Tests use synthetic PDFs in isolated storage only; these are never installed as live templates.
- Known condo, multi-family, mixed-use and commercial records cannot start preparation or purchase fulfillment. Existing documents remain downloadable. Unknown imported classification requires the owner's explicit single-family confirmation.

- DocuSign is still configured for **demo**. Demo fulfillment requires Stripe test mode and cannot dispatch real mail. Provider credentials and live setup remain necessary. The separate administrator Stripe checkout test does not create a DocuSign or mailing order.
- Mailing and signature-method acceptance require the existing fulfillment review. User has authorized assuming DocuSign acceptance for this pilot; county acceptance has not been confirmed.
- No automated submission, automatic postal dispatch, appraisal, title verification, legal representation, signature verification of uploads, or notarial service is claimed.
- PDFs/JPGs/PNGs only. Individual uploads: 15 MB; evidence aggregate: 25 MB; assembled packet must fit the existing 15 MB packet-upload limit. PDFs are capped at 100 pages per attachment and 150 combined pages. Encrypted, corrupt or missing files fail assembly. Existing signed original uploads remain separately downloadable; merged PDF pages are copies.
- Evidence removal changes the working manifest without deleting historical R2 objects. Approved packet snapshots remain immutable. Revisions require reapproval and active DocuSign envelopes must be voided first.
- Third-party library: pinned MIT-licensed pdf-lib 1.17.1, vendored locally. No runtime dependency download.
