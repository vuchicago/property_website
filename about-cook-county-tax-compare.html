About Cook County Tax Compare

Cook County Tax Compare is a property assessment comparison tool and optional appeal-help service for Cook County residential property owners.

What We Do

We help homeowners compare their Cook County property assessment against nearby properties, review official appeal timing, and decide whether an appeal may be worth exploring.

What We Do Not Guarantee

We do not guarantee a lower assessment, tax savings, refund, or successful appeal. Cook County agencies make appeal decisions based on their own rules, deadlines, and evidence standards.

Data Sources

The site is designed around public Cook County property and assessment data. Users should verify any final figures, deadlines, and filing requirements with official county sources.
