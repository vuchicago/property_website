What The Tool Does

Cook County Property Tax Assessment Comparison

The Property Tax Comparison Tool helps homeowners compare Cook County property taxes and assessments by address. It is designed to make public assessment data easier to review before checking official county records or preparing an appeal.

Find Nearby Comparables

Search for a Cook County property and review nearby parcels that may be useful for comparing assessed value, building size, property class, and location.

Review Assessment Signals

Use the comparison view to spot whether your property appears higher than similar nearby properties. The tool is a research starting point, not a guarantee of savings.

Prepare For Next Steps

After comparing properties, confirm the PIN, appeal window, exemptions, and filing rules with official Cook County sources before submitting an appeal.
